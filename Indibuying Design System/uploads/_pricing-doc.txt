Pricing
How we price
We price by the work, not by the package. Pick the parts of the sourcing process where you want our help, and pay only for those. No retainers, no commitments, no hidden markups.
Workshop production costs are paid by you directly to the workshop at their quoted price. We don&apos;t take supplier commissions or add margins.

The three work units
Most clients use one, two, or all three of these. They&apos;re sequenced — most useful when used in order — but you can engage us for any single piece independently.

How a typical engagement runs
Most first-time buyers use all three work units in sequence over 8-12 weeks:
Week 1-4: Vendor Search + Sampling Coordination ($250). We find the workshop, you approve the sample.
Week 5-10: Production runs. You pay the workshop directly. We coordinate during production, then do the pre-shipment QC visit ($150) on dispatch day.
Week 11-12: Packaging, Logistics &amp; Documentation Support ($250). We handle the shipment logistics. You receive your order.
Total fees for the full engagement: $650, plus actual workshop production costs and shipping (paid by you directly).

Common questions
Can I use just one work unit?
Yes. If you already have a workshop and just want pre-shipment QC, you can engage us only for Work Unit 2. If your workshop handles their own shipping but you need documentation help, you can engage us only for Work Unit 3. Pick what you need.
Do you mark up workshop prices?
No. You pay the workshop directly at their quoted price. Our fees come from you only.
What if the QC finds defects?
We coordinate rework with the workshop before dispatch. If a second QC visit is needed after rework, it&apos;s billed at $150 (or actuals if outside Jaipur). Most orders don&apos;t require a second visit.
Do you do retainers for ongoing production?
Yes — for brands running multiple production cycles per quarter, a monthly retainer often makes more sense than per-order pricing. Mention it on the fit call and we&apos;ll scope it based on your volume.
Are there refunds if a vendor doesn&apos;t work out?
On Work Unit 1 (Vendor Search), if we can&apos;t surface a workshop you&apos;d actually order from after the sample round, we refund the second half ($125). The first half covers our research time and is non-refundable.
What categories do you cover at these prices?
Jewellery is our deepest network. We also handle handicrafts, home furnishings, furniture, and handcrafted apparel where the workshop is in our network or accessible from Jaipur. For categories outside Jaipur, the QC unit may include travel costs.

Get started
A 20-minute fit call to scope which work units you need. We&apos;ll confirm pricing for your specific case (especially if your workshop is outside Jaipur) before any engagement starts.

Buyer India Tour Support
If you&apos;re flying in to India to meet factories and see production first-hand, we make the trip count. Most buyers come for 3-4 days and want to visit as many vetted suppliers as possible without wasting time on logistics, navigation, or factories that turn out not to fit. That&apos;s the whole job.
We schedule the meetings, plan the routes, handle ground logistics, translate at factory visits, and travel with you through the trip. You land, we drive, you go home with decisions made.
What&apos;s included

Translation at factory visits — Hindi, Marwari, regional languages as needed
Real-time negotiation support — pricing, MOQs, payment terms in the room
Post-meeting debrief notes — capability fit, red flags, our honest read on each supplier
End-of-trip summary with shortlist and recommended next steps

How this differs from a virtual assistant
A $10/hour VA in the Philippines or India does email coordination. We do on-ground operations. The difference matters when:
You need someone to physically walk into a factory and look at production
You need someone who speaks Hindi/Marwari/Kannada to negotiate with karigars and karkhana owners
You need someone who knows whether a Jaipur quote is fair or a new-buyer premium
You need someone who can spot the difference between real 925 silver and 800-grade with a fake stamp
You need someone who can navigate Indian export compliance without learning it from scratch
A VA can chase emails. We can chase outcomes.

Common questions
What&apos;s the minimum engagement?
No formal minimum. Single-task engagements as small as 2-3 hours are fine. Most projects fall in the 5-20 hour range.
Are travel costs included?
Local travel within Jaipur city is included in the hourly rate. Outside-city travel (Jodhpur, Delhi, Mumbai, Kerala, etc.) is billed at actuals — train/flight + accommodation + ground transport. We share an estimate before any outside-city engagement.
How do you bill time?
Tracked in 15-minute increments with a brief work log per session. Shared with you weekly or at project completion. No padding, no minimum block billing.
Can you sign an NDA?
Yes — standard for buyer engagements. We sign before any work begins. Specific vendor names, designs, or sourcing strategies stay confidential.
Do you offer this for non-sourcing operations work?
If it&apos;s India operations work that benefits from a person on the ground — yes, in many cases. Examples: market research visits, partner due diligence, exhibition coverage, vendor onboarding for non-manufacturing services. Ask on the fit call.
How much notice do you need for an India tour visit?
Ideally 3-4 weeks. That gives us time to shortlist factories, confirm meetings, and plan logistics properly. We&apos;ve handled trips at 7-10 days&apos; notice when needed, but the factory line-up is tighter and some preferred suppliers may not have availability that quickly.
How does this differ from your sourcing services?
Sourcing services are packaged engagements with fixed deliverables (vendor search, QC, retainer). Operations support is hourly, flexible, and unbundled — you direct the work as needed. Many buyers use both, depending on the situation.
