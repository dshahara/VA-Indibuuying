The work, specifically

Recurring inventory and procurement work that has to be right every week.

Inventory Reconciliation

Daily or weekly stock counts checked against system inventory. Shortages, overages, stockouts, slow movers, and phantom stock flagged with quantity and value.

Receiving and Billing Audits

PO against receiver against packing list against vendor invoice. Customer order against shipment against invoice. This is where incorrect pricing, missing invoices, duplicate transactions, shortages, and overages usually get caught.

Purchase Orders

PO creation from approved templates, replenishment quantities from sales velocity, open PO tracking, supplier lead time monitoring, and follow-up on late or partial orders.

ERP Hygiene

Keeping your system aligned with physical stock, open POs, receivers, shipments, invoices, and vendor records, so the numbers you use to make decisions are the real ones.

Supplier Follow-Up

Sample status, QC documents, production updates, vendor records, and inbound material readiness. Hindi and regional language follow-up where that speeds things up.

Inbound and Freight Coordination

Pickup status, shipment milestones, clearance documents, delivery timelines, and early escalation when a production or shipment date is going to slip.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

Do I need SOPs ready?

No. If you have SOPs, we follow them. If not, we start with your current process and help turn it into a simple repeatable workflow.

Which systems can you work with?

We can work with inventory systems, ERPs, ecommerce platforms, spreadsheets, and shared folders. Common examples include Zoho Inventory, Shopify, Amazon, ApparelMagic, Katana, Excel, and Google Sheets.

Is the assistant dedicated?

Yes. Monthly plans include a dedicated assistant assigned to your workflow.

What if the assistant is not a fit?

Indibuying provides oversight and replacement support. We also maintain handover notes so the work does not depend on one person’s memory.

Can you work with suppliers outside India?

Yes. The service is built for import-led businesses working with overseas suppliers, including India, China, Southeast Asia, and other regions.

How do I know the work is being done?

You receive a clear output log. For discrepancies, we document what was checked, what was found, the quantity or value involved, and the suggested next action.
